Stratagem Trainer catalog data

The directional codes are adapted from the community-maintained Helldivers 2
Stratagem JSON Generator by Keebs, pinned to commit
eda04ed2f6a7a97f7253c21454f2926cf7c26c3f:
https://github.com/k33bs/Helldivers-2-Stratagem-JSON-Generator

Additional factual sources, verification scope, and corrections are recorded
in docs/catalog.md in the source distribution. Individual packs include their
source links. This is a community trainer and is not affiliated with Sony,
PlayStation, or Arrowhead Game Studios.
